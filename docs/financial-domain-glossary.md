# Financial & Accounts Payable Domain Glossary

This reference clarifies standard accounts payable terms, procurement workflows, and reconciliation mathematics for Zamp ASA.

## 1. Core Terminology

- **Accounts Payable (AP)**: The organizational department and accounting ledger responsible for tracking, validating, and settling monetary obligations owed to commercial vendors and suppliers.
- **Purchase Order (PO)**: A legally binding commercial document issued by a buyer (Zamp ASA) to a vendor, indicating types, quantities, and agreed prices for products or services.
- **Goods Received Note (GRN)**: A formal record generated by warehouse or receiving personnel certifying the physical delivery, receipt date, condition, and actual quantity of goods supplied against a specific PO line.
- **Vendor Master Record**: The authoritative registry of pre-approved suppliers, containing registered legal entities, VAT/Tax identification numbers, authorized payment terms, and active PO contracts.
- **Line Item Matching**: The granular comparison between an invoice row item (SKU/description, quantity, unit price) and its corresponding PO line item.

## 2. Matching Methodologies

### Two-Way Matching (2-Way)
- Applied to: Professional services, software licenses, consulting, digital utilities, or non-tangible deliverables where no physical warehouse receiving takes place.
- Comparison:
  1. **Invoice vs Purchase Order**:
     - Vendor Identity (Invoice Vendor == PO Vendor)
     - Unit Price (Invoice Unit Price <= PO Unit Price + Tolerance)
     - Billed Quantity (Invoice Quantity <= Unallocated PO Quantity)
     - Line Total (Invoice Total == Quantity * Unit Price)

### Three-Way Matching (3-Way)
- Applied to: Physical inventory, hardware, raw materials, office equipment, and tangible shipments.
- Comparison:
  1. **Invoice vs Purchase Order**: Checks authorized price, vendor, and terms.
  2. **Invoice vs Goods Received Note (GRN)**: Checks that billed quantity does not exceed verified received quantity.
  3. **PO vs GRN**: Ensures the GRN references the authentic PO and lines.
- Invariant: A 3-way match cannot succeed without a valid GRN matching the delivery.

## 3. Allocation & Balance Tracking

- **Committed PO Balance**: The cumulative sum of quantities/values already approved across previous invoices against this PO.
- **Unallocated PO Balance**: `PO Total Capacity - Sum(Approved Historical Allocations)`.
- **Pending Allocation**: Temporary reservation while an invoice is in verification.
- **Safety Invariant**: Only invoices in status `APPROVED` or `APPROVED_WITH_TOLERANCE` permanently consume PO balance. Invoices placed on `HOLD_FOR_REVIEW` or `BLOCKED` release or never commit unallocated balances.

## 4. Price Tolerance Rules
- **Acceptable Variance**: A pre-authorized variance percentage (default: `2.0%` / `0.02`) applied to unit price fluctuations (e.g., minor fuel surcharges or currency conversion fractions).
- **Calculation Formula**:
  $$\text{Variance \%} = \frac{|\text{Invoice Unit Price} - \text{PO Unit Price}|}{\text{PO Unit Price}} \times 100$$
- If $\text{Variance \%} \le 2.0\%$, the invoice can receive `APPROVED_WITH_TOLERANCE` provided all other constraints pass.
- If $\text{Variance \%} > 2.0\%$, it triggers `BLOCKED` (Overbilling).
